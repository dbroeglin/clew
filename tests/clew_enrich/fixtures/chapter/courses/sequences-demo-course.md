# Sequences

## Geometric sequences

A geometric sequence with common ratio $q$ satisfies $u_{n+1}=qu_n$.
Its general term is $u_n=u_0q^n$.

## Geometric limits

If $|q|<1$, then $q^n\to 0$. For every real constant $a$, $aq^n\to 0$.

## Monotone convergence

A nondecreasing sequence bounded above converges to a finite limit.
To investigate monotonicity, calculate $u_{n+1}-u_n$.
A bound valid for every $n$ can be proved by induction.

## Passing to the limit

If $u_n\to\ell$, $u_{n+1}=f(u_n)$ and $f$ is continuous at $\ell$,
then $\ell=f(\ell)$. Convergence must be established first.
