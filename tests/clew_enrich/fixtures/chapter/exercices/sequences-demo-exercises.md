# Sequence worksheet

## Exercise 1

For every $n\in\mathbb{N}$, let $u_n=7(2/5)^n$.
Justify each answer.

1. Show that $(u_n)$ is geometric and identify its common ratio.

2. Determine the limit of $(u_n)$.

## Exercise 2

Consider the sequence defined by:

$$
v_0=1,\qquad v_{n+1}=\frac{v_n+3}{2}.
$$

1. Show that $1\leq v_n\leq 3$ for every $n$, then show that the sequence is nondecreasing.

2. Deduce that the sequence converges and calculate its limit.
