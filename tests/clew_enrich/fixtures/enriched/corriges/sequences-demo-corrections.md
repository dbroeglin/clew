# Sequence worksheet corrections

## Exercise 1

> [!reponse] Exercise 1 - Answer 1
> [question:: [[sequences-demo-exercises#^q-ex1-1]]]
> 1. $u_{n+1}=(2/5)u_n$: the common ratio is $2/5$.
>

^r-ex1-1

> [!reponse] Exercise 1 - Answer 2
> [question:: [[sequences-demo-exercises#^q-ex1-2]]]
> 2. Since $|2/5|<1$, $(2/5)^n\to 0$, so $u_n\to 0$.
>

^r-ex1-2

## Exercise 2

> [!reponse] Exercise 2 - Answer 1

>    $$

>    $$
