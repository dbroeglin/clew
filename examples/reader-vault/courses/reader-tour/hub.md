# Course reader tour

This small, synthetic course demonstrates Clew's reader. It contains no learner
records and does not need to be registered in Obsidian.

Use **Next** to follow the course's authored order. The same page can also be
opened through a wikilink: [[01-mathematics|Mathematics in Markdown]].

## A shared reading surface

The session can move this reader to another page or refresh it after a course
edit. You can move through the course yourself without sending a chat message.

Expand **Contents** to jump to a heading on the current page. It starts
collapsed and keeps your open/closed choice while you navigate.

Select a passage and choose **Ask in chat** to add it as an attachment in the
chat composer. Write your question there, then send it yourself. The reader
does not send anything automatically.

> Course source files stay unchanged. A reading position is not evidence that
> you have learned, skipped, or mastered a concept.
