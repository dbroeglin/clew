# Mechanics of everyday motion

<!-- clew:nav -->
Originals: [[courses/mechanics/sources/mechanics-notes.pdf#page=1|mechanics-notes.pdf, page 1]]
<!-- /clew:nav -->

A short synthetic course that describes motion before explaining it. It shares
the idea of a ratio with [[courses/proportional-reasoning/course|Proportional
reasoning]], so the two courses are usually imported together.

## Reading this course

Use **Next** and **Previous** to follow the authored order. The order is the one
recorded in each note's frontmatter, so the reader never guesses a next page
from the directory listing. Expand **Contents** to jump to a heading on the
current page; it starts collapsed and keeps your choice while you navigate.

Select a passage and choose **Ask in chat** to stage it as an attachment in the
composer. Write your question there and send it yourself. Navigating or
refreshing stages nothing.

> A reading position is not evidence that you have learned, skipped, or
> mastered a concept. Nothing in this course directory is a learner record.
