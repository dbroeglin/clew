# Ratio

## Definition

A ratio compares two quantities by dividing one by the other. For quantities
$a$ and $b \neq 0$, the ratio $a : b$ is the number $a / b$. Multiplying both
quantities by the same non-zero $k$ leaves it unchanged, so $a : b$ and
$ka : kb$ are the same ratio.
