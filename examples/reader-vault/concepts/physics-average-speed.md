# Average speed

## Definition

The average speed over an interval is the total distance covered divided by the
total time taken, $\bar v = \Delta s / \Delta t$. It is a rate rather than a
plain ratio, because the two quantities are of different kinds, so it carries
the unit of distance over the unit of time.
